# Email/SMS Spam Classifier

This project uses Machine Learning to classify messages as **Spam** or **Not Spam**.

## Test Messages

### Spam Examples

1. Congratulations! You have won a $1,000 Walmart gift card. Click the link now to claim your prize.
2. URGENT! Your account has been selected for a cash reward of $5,000. Call now to claim.
3. You have won a FREE iPhone! Send your details to receive your prize.
4. Claim your exclusive FREE vacation package today. Call 0800123456 now!
5. Congratulations! You are the lucky winner of £5000. Reply WIN to claim your cash prize.
6. Your mobile number has won a cash prize of $2,000. Call immediately to collect your reward.
7. URGENT: Your bank account will be suspended. Verify your details immediately using the link below.
8. You have been selected for a special loan offer. Get $10,000 instantly with no paperwork. Apply now!

### Not Spam Examples

1. Hey, are you coming to the office tomorrow?
2. Hi, please call me when you are free.
3. Don't forget to bring your documents to the meeting tomorrow.
4. I am running late. I will reach home in about 20 minutes.
5. Can you please send me the notes from today's class?
6. Your appointment is scheduled for tomorrow at 10 AM.
7. Hi Mom, I reached the office safely.
8. The meeting has been moved to 3 PM today.
9. Please buy some milk while coming home.
10. I will call you after I finish my work.
